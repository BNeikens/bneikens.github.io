About Neikens Electrical Contracting

A Practical Partner for Complex Electrical Construction

Neikens Electrical Contracting is a North Carolina-based electrical contractor focused on industrial, municipal, civil, and critical-infrastructure projects.

We understand that successful plant and infrastructure work requires more than installing conduit and pulling wire. It requires careful review of plans and specifications, coordination with general contractors and equipment vendors, attention to shutdown constraints, disciplined documentation, and field leadership that can solve problems without losing sight of safety or schedule.

Our goal is to be the electrical contractor owners, engineers, and general contractors can rely on for dependable estimating, clear communication, and technically sound execution.

Why Project Teams Choose NEC

  • Industrial focus: Experience with process equipment, motors, controls, distribution, generators, and demanding operating environments.
  • Field-informed estimating: Scope review grounded in installation methods, access, sequencing, temporary conditions, and commissioning needs.
  • Responsive coordination: Direct communication with owners, engineers, general contractors, vendors, and other trades.
  • Safety and reliability: Work planned around energized systems, operating facilities, outages, and critical-service requirements.
